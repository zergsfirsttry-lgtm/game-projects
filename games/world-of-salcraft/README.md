# World of Salcraft

*A pixel-art WoW-inspired roguelike RPG, played entirely in your browser.*

**[▶ Play World of Salcraft](https://zergsfirsttry-lgtm.github.io/game-projects/games/world-of-salcraft/)**

No install, no account, no download. Pick a class, descend into an
ever-deepening, ever-harder world, and build up a permanent Sanctuary of
gear, gold, professions, and companions that carries forward no matter how
far any one adventurer makes it.

## The core loop

Every adventure starts the same way: choose a class, then set out onto a
branching path of encounters - battles, elites, rest sites, shops, treasure,
strange events, and the occasional legendary or rare creature. Win fights,
spend gold, gear up, and push toward the act boss waiting at the end of the
path. Clear it, and the world pushes back harder on the next act - deeper,
tougher, and dressed in a new backdrop entirely (nine WoW-flavored zones,
from a quiet starting forest all the way to the Twisting Nether).

Die, and the run ends - but almost nothing is truly lost. Character level,
bank gold, gear, professions, reputation, and companions all live in your
permanent **Sanctuary**, untouched by any one run's death. Only what you were
carrying in-pocket for that specific attempt goes with you.

Flip on **AUTO** at any point and the whole loop plays itself: your character
fights, picks the hardest path forward, and keeps adventuring on its own,
with quick toast notifications standing in for the usual victory screens.
Close the tab entirely and time still passes - come back later to a
**Welcome Back** summary of everything earned while you were away.

## Progression

- **Character level** - the usual RPG climb, up to 99, unlocking talent
  points and raw power along the way.
- **Talents** - three specialization trees per class, WoW-style, each with
  its own build identity.
- **Gear** - nineteen equip slots (weapons, armor, and accessories), rarity
  from Common up to Legendary, found on adventures, crafted, or bought.
  Filling more slots with better rarity grows a Gear Set Bonus, a global %
  multiplier (up to +100%) on top of everything your items, relics, food,
  and pets/mounts already grant. Enchanting turns unwanted gear into
  materials (Disenchant) and spends them adding a passive ability to
  whatever you've got equipped (Enchant).
- **Professions** - eight gathering/crafting trades (mining, herbalism,
  fishing, cooking, and more) that level independently and interconnect with
  each other.
- **Reputation** - a standing with each of the nine zones, WoW-style
  (Neutral → Friendly → Honored → Revered → Exalted), earned by completing
  encounters and paying off in permanent gold bonuses.
- **Companions & pets/mounts** - tame pets and mounts that fight alongside
  you and level up in the House, or recruit up to four real player
  characters (imported from someone else's save file) into your party for
  a shared adventure.
- **PvP** - duel a mirror of your own build, or brave a randomly-geared
  Rival Ghost for Honor and rare loot.
- **Dungeons & Raids** - ten tiers of solo dungeons and full-party raids, far
  tougher than the open road.

## Systems at a glance

Nine classes, each with a unique class-defining skill and full weapon-type
authenticity (a Rogue can't pick up a two-handed maul; a Warlock isn't
swinging a sword). Relics and curses shape a single run's build on the fly.
Speed does double duty in combat - it still sets turn order, but it also
rolls the chance a basic Attack chains into a combo, swinging again before
the enemy replies. A full crafting/cooking economy turns gathered materials
into gear and temporary buffs. Every sprite - every class, every monster,
every pet and mount - is procedurally-generated pixel art, no external image
assets required.

Jump in, pick a class, and the game will walk you through your first few
steps itself.
